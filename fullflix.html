AI-POWERED FULFILMENT

Intelligence built into every order

FULFIL.X isn't just a warehouse. Machine learning runs across our operation, forecasting demand, routing parcels and catching errors. Your fulfilment gets faster and sharper the more you ship.

01

Demand forecasting

Our models read your sales trends and seasonality to predict what you'll ship next, so stock and staffing are ready before the orders land.

02

Smart carrier routing

Every order is matched to the best carrier and service in real time, balancing speed and cost against live network performance.

03

Accuracy checks at pack

Vision-assisted verification at the pack bench catches mis-picks before they leave the building, protecting your 99.94% accuracy.

04

Anomaly detection

The system flags unusual stock movements, delays and spikes the moment they appear, so issues are handled before they reach your customer.
